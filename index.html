What we help with

From a few hours of daily support to full live-in nursing, we match the right caregiver to your household's needs.

On-Site Health Screening

We bring blood pressure checks, glucose testing, and general screening directly to companies, churches, and community groups across Kumasi.

Daily Living Support

Help with bathing, dressing, meal preparation, and mobility, so clients stay comfortable and independent at home.

Medication Reminders

Structured reminders and light medication management so prescriptions are taken correctly and on time.

Vital Signs Monitoring

Regular tracking of blood pressure, pulse, temperature, and oxygen levels, with concerns flagged early.

Live-In & Live-Out Nursing

Registered nurses available around the clock or on a scheduled basis, depending on your household's needs.

Elderly & Disability Care

Compassionate, dignity-first support for seniors and people living with disabilities, tailored to each client.

Care that travels to you

Our team and screening van reach clients directly — at home, at the office, or at a community gathering — so getting proper care doesn't depend on getting to a hospital.

Every visit is carried out by a registered, background-checked member of our team, briefed on your specific care plan before they arrive.
