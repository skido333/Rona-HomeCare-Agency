About Us

Care that treats every client like family.

Rona Home Care Agency provides in-home health and personal care services to families across Adum-Kumasi and the surrounding Kotwi area — for people who want to stay safe, healthy, and independent at home.

Our approach

We work with individuals who need daily support with everyday tasks, help managing medication, or full nursing care — whether that's a few hours a week or round-the-clock attention.

Our team includes registered nurses and trained caregivers who bring hospital-level attention into a home setting, so clients get professional care without leaving the comfort of familiar surroundings.

Beyond individual home care, we also work directly with companies, churches, and communities to run on-site health screenings — bringing checkups and vital signs monitoring to people where they already gather.

What guides our work

Family-first care

Every client is treated with the same care and respect we'd want for our own families — not as a case number.

The right match

We pair the right caregiver to the right need, whether that's a few hours of support or live-in nursing.

Open communication

We keep families informed at every step, not just the client — because care decisions are rarely made alone.
