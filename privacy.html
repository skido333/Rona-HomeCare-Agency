Rona Home Care Agency ("Rona," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide through our inquiry form, such as your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Care-related information you choose to share when describing the type of care you are seeking.
  • Basic technical data, such as browser type and pages visited, collected automatically to help us maintain the site.

How we use this information

  • To respond to inquiries and arrange care services.
  • To communicate with you about appointments, staff, or billing related to services you request.
  • To improve this website and our services.

How we protect your information

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal and health-related information you share with us, and we limit access to staff who need it to provide your care.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with staff assigned to your care, or where required by law.

Your choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to request a correction, or to ask that it be deleted, subject to any record-keeping obligations required in the provision of health-related care.
